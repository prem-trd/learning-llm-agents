Nimbus Files — Pricing Plans

Nimbus Files offers three subscription tiers: Free, Pro, and Business.

The Free plan includes 5 GB of storage, file sharing with view-only links, and
support for files up to 250 MB in size. It is intended for individuals trying
out the product and does not include version history.

The Pro plan costs $9 per month (or $90 per year, a 17% discount) and includes
2 TB of storage, unlimited file size, 30 days of version history, and the
ability to share files with edit permissions. Pro is our most popular plan
for freelancers and small teams.

The Business plan costs $22 per user per month and includes unlimited
storage, 180 days of version history, single sign-on (SSO), an admin console
for managing team members, and priority support. Business requires a minimum
of 3 seats.

All plans can be upgraded, downgraded, or cancelled at any time from the
account settings page. Downgrading takes effect at the end of the current
billing cycle; there is no partial credit for unused time.
