Nimbus Files — Refund Policy

We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee on all new Pro and Business
subscriptions. If you are not satisfied within the first 14 days after your
initial purchase, contact support and we will issue a full refund to your
original payment method. Refunds are typically processed within 5 to 7
business days.

Renewals are not covered by the 14-day guarantee. If your subscription
auto-renews and you did not intend to renew, you have 72 hours from the
renewal charge to request a refund of that specific renewal payment.

Refunds are not available for the Business plan once any team member has
used a Business-only feature (such as SSO configuration) during the current
billing period, since that usage cannot be reversed.

To request a refund, email billing@nimbusfiles.example with your account
email and the reason for the request. We do not require you to state a
reason, but it helps us improve the product.

Downgrading to the Free plan does not trigger a refund for the remaining
time on a paid plan; see the Pricing document for how downgrades work.
