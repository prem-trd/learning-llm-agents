Nimbus Files — Security & Compliance

All files stored on Nimbus Files are encrypted at rest using AES-256, and all
data in transit between your device and our servers is encrypted using
TLS 1.3. Encryption keys are managed by our infrastructure provider and
rotated automatically every 90 days.

Nimbus Files is SOC 2 Type II certified. Our latest audit report is available
to Business plan customers upon request through the admin console.

We are GDPR compliant for customers in the European Union. Business plan
customers can request a Data Processing Addendum (DPA) from their account
manager. We do not sell customer data to third parties under any
circumstances.

Two-factor authentication (2FA) is available on all plans and can be enabled
from account security settings. We strongly recommend enabling 2FA,
particularly for Business plan admin accounts, since admins can access every
team member's files.

Business plan customers can additionally enforce SSO via SAML 2.0, restrict
login to specific IP ranges, and set a mandatory session timeout for all
users on the account.

Nimbus Files does not currently support HIPAA-compliant storage and should
not be used to store protected health information.
