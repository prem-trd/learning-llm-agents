Nimbus Files — Support Channels & Hours

Free plan customers can reach support through our community forum and the
help center at help.nimbusfiles.example. Response times on the forum are
best-effort and not guaranteed.

Pro plan customers get email support at support@nimbusfiles.example, with a
target first response time of one business day. Support is available Monday
through Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM Eastern Time, excluding US federal holidays.

Business plan customers get priority email and live chat support, available
Monday through Friday, 6 AM to 10 PM Eastern Time. Business customers with
10 or more seats are assigned a dedicated account manager and can request a
30-minute onboarding call.

We do not currently offer 24/7 phone support on any plan. Critical outages
affecting all customers are posted to status.nimbusfiles.example and updated
at least every 30 minutes until resolved.

For billing questions specifically, use billing@nimbusfiles.example rather
than the general support address — billing requests are routed to a
different team and get a faster response there.
